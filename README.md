ShopSmart Retail Sales Analysis

This is an Excel analysis of ShopSmart, a Nigerian retail chain built from nine datasets: customers, orders, order_items, products, stores, suppliers, returns, inventory and employees. The aim was to clean the data, measure business performance and give management clear recommendations.

What I did

I cleaned the data by removing duplicates, checking for missing values, fixing date formats and making currency fields numeric. I converted each dataset into an Excel Table and linked them with lookup formulas. I calculated the main KPIs: total revenue, order and customer counts, average order value, return rate and inventory value. I built pivot tables and charts for sales by product, category, store, region and month. I designed an executive dashboard with KPI cards and slicers and exported it as a PDF. Finally, I wrote a business report with findings, recommendations, a risk and an opportunity.

Key results

ShopSmart earned 7.58B from 50,000 orders in 2025, about 151,505 per order. Beauty is the top category at 23.3% of revenue, and the South is the top region at 32.0%. Lagos, Ibadan and Abuja are the top stores and together make 48.5% of revenue. Monthly sales are steady, from 567M in February to 661M in December. About 5% of orders are returned, with Home and Beauty highest at 5.1% each. Enugu, Kano and Awka hold the least stock.

Recommendations

First, invest in Beauty while watching product quality. Second, reduce returns in Home and Beauty. Third, review stock levels at Enugu, Kano and Awka.

Risk: sales depend heavily on three stores.
Opportunity: grow the weaker stores and the West region.
